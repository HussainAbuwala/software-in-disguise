# Software in Disguise

**Software in Disguise** is a YouTube Shorts series for **The Unplanned Stack**. Hussain, on camera, takes something
viewers do in apps every day (a glitch, a delay, a feature they never thought about) and shows the software working
behind it. The friendly app screen is the disguise.

From October 2026 the series is Hussain to camera with screen recordings, props and simple diagrams, modelled on
[candlesan](https://www.youtube.com/@candlesan/shorts). The earlier format (code-drawn cartoon stories with synthetic
voices) is retired; see `EPISODES.md`.

## Start here

1. Read [`SERIES_GUIDE.md`](SERIES_GUIDE.md): the promise, the visual toolkit, and the lessons from our analytics.
2. Pick a moment from the idea bank in [`EPISODES.md`](EPISODES.md).
3. Copy [`templates/episode-brief.md`](templates/episode-brief.md) into `episodes/NNN-slug/BRIEF.md`.
