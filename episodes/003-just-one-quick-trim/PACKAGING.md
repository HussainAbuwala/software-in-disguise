## Description

He was promised the next haircut. Then came one beard, one neckline, and one moustache—each quick enough to go first.

That is how starvation can happen in software: other jobs keep receiving priority while one waiting job never gets its turn. A fair scheduler makes sure waiting work eventually gets a chance to run.
