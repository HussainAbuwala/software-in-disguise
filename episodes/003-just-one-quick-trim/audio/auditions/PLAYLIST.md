# Voice audition playlist

All candidates use the same text within their role and comparable peak levels. Neutral labels appear in filenames; the underlying Kokoro voice IDs and settings are recorded in `manifest.json`.

## Role comparison files

- `comparison-groom.wav`: G1, G2, G3
- `comparison-barber.wav`: B1, B2, B3
- `comparison-narrator.wav`: N1, N2
- `comparison-supporting.wav`: C1A, C1B, C2A, C2B, C3A, C3B
- `comparison-reel.wav`: all four role comparisons, followed by chemistry P1 and P2

## Chemistry scenes

- `chemistry-P1-restrained.wav`: G1 + B1 + C1B
- `chemistry-P2-warmer.wav`: G3 + B2 + C1B

Each chemistry file contains the opening request, the barber's promise, the first interruption, the barber's exception, and the final question. The words remain unchanged across the two versions.
