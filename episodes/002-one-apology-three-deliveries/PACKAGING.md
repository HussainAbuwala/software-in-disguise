## Description

Idempotency explained through an apology that accidentally becomes three flower deliveries.

He orders one small bouquet. The confirmation never arrives. Two retries later, his promise not to overdo things takes an unfortunate turn.

In software, an idempotent operation can be retried without creating extra effects. For this kind of order, the retries need to refer to the same intended request—often using a stable idempotency key. A missing confirmation does not necessarily mean the original order failed.
